# Sales CRM – Customer Relationship Management System

## Project Overview

The **Sales CRM** is a centralized Customer Relationship Management platform designed to streamline an organization's complete sales lifecycle, from prospect identification and lead generation to opportunity management, customer engagement, and deal closure.

The platform provides a structured environment for managing prospects, leads, sales activities, follow-ups, communication, field visits, sales pipelines, and performance analytics.

The system focuses on improving sales team productivity, maintaining organized customer information, tracking sales activities, and providing management with better visibility into the overall sales process.

## Core Modules

The platform consists of the following modules:

- **Dashboard:** Centralized overview of sales activities, lead performance, pipeline status, follow-ups, and key business KPIs.
- **Prospect Management:** Bulk prospect uploads, prospect assignment, ownership transfer, qualification, and conversion into leads.
- **Lead Management:** Lead creation, assignment, tracking, categorization, tagging, and customer information management.
- **Outreach Gateway:** Centralized communication management through calls, emails, WhatsApp, and lead engagement activities.
- **Follow-ups:** Follow-up scheduling, reminders, activity tracking, and pending follow-up management.
- **Opportunity & Pipeline Management:** Opportunity tracking, sales stages, deal progress, and conversion monitoring.
- **Daily Execution:** Employee task management, daily activity tracking, task progress, and managerial review.
- **Call Log:** Centralized call activity records and communication history.
- **Field Visit Management:** Visit planning, location-based coordination, meeting records, Minutes of Meeting (MOM), visit outcomes, and expense tracking.
- **Team Management:** Employee management, team assignments, and sales activity monitoring.
- **Intelligence Directory:** Centralized business and customer intelligence information.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Sales performance reporting, lead analysis, team productivity, pipeline insights, and activity-based analytics.
- **Masters:** Centralized configuration of dropdown values, categories, stages, and other reusable system data.

## User Roles

The system supports role-based access and management.

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Overall platform administration and system-level configuration. |
| Admin | Manages CRM configuration, users, teams, and operational settings. |
| Manager | Monitors team performance, manages assignments, and reviews sales activities. |
| Employee / Sales Executive | Manages assigned prospects, leads, follow-ups, opportunities, and daily sales activities. |

## Key System Capabilities

- Centralized prospect and lead management.
- Structured prospect-to-lead conversion.
- Lead assignment and ownership transfer.
- Automated lead distribution through round-robin assignment.
- Follow-up scheduling and activity tracking.
- Centralized calling, email, and WhatsApp communication management.
- Sales pipeline and opportunity tracking.
- Daily task and execution monitoring.
- Field visit planning and reporting.
- Team-level performance monitoring.
- Centralized customer and business intelligence.
- Sales dashboards and management reporting.
- Role-based access control.
- Configurable CRM masters and settings.

## Important Implementation Notes

- Prospects are managed separately before being converted into leads.
- Lead distribution supports automated round-robin assignment based on configured business rules.
- Outreach activities are integrated with lead management.
- Field visit records include meeting details, outcomes, MOM, and expenses.
- Daily Execution provides visibility into employee tasks and work progress.
- CRM pricing and subscription configuration are managed according to the defined commercial plans.
- Certain communication and external service capabilities depend on their respective integrations.
